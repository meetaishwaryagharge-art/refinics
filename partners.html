Who is a DSA?

A Direct Selling Agent (DSA) is an efficient link that channels communication between financial institutions like banks and NBFCs. Loan DSA Agents are assigned key duties, including:

Identifying Potential Customers

Searching and connecting with the individuals who need financial services.

Guiding Borrowers

Educating customers on financial products and how to apply for a loan.

Ensuring Documentation

Ensuring the accuracy and availability of all necessary paperwork.

Closing Deals

Connect borrowers with lenders and finalize transactions.

As a DSA, you can offer an extensive range of financial products such as home loans, business loans, personal loans, loans against property, car loans, education loans, solar loans, machinery loans, gold loans, credit cards, and insurance products. You can earn payouts based on the loan amount disbursed.
